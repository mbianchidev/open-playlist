Music without walls

One playlist format.
Every music service.

Open Playlist is an open format and API for music playlists. Any music service can adopt it, so you can move and share playlists between services without rebuilding them or getting locked in.

Why it matters

Playlists take time to build. They should not stay behind when you change music service.

Own your playlists

Export, migrate and share playlists on your terms, not the platform's.

Switch without starting over

Move between services and keep the playlists, liked tracks and order you already have.

Room for new services

An open format lets new apps and tools read and write the playlists people already own.

How it works

Providers implement adapters that translate between their own playlist data and the Open Playlist format. Four core endpoints move playlists in and out.

1

Adopt the format

Map your playlist and track data to the Open Playlist schema.

2

Import and export

Bring playlists in from one provider and write them out to another.

3

Match across services

ISRC codes, provider URIs and fuzzy matching keep tracks aligned between services.

GET /open-playlists/{userId} GET /open-playlists/{playlistId} POST /open-playlists/import POST /open-playlists/export

Version 0.2.0 adds local playlist-file imports, CSV, TXT, M3U8, XSPF and JSON file exports, and bulk playlist organization with preflight checks and retries. See the API reference for every endpoint.

A simple, extensible data model

Playlists and tracks are at the core. Albums and artists are first-class library entities. Every object is provider-neutral and carries extensible metadata.

Playlist

  • name · tracks · required
  • id · description · photo
  • owner_id · owner_name
  • is_owned · is_followed · collaborative
  • kind · standard playlist or liked tracks
  • snapshot_id · created_at · updated_at

Track

  • title · artist · required
  • album · duration_s · isrc
  • release_date · release_year · genre
  • track_number · disc_number · explicit
  • composer · credits · label
  • media_type · is_local · added_at
  • provider_uris · cross-service links
  • metadata · extensible key-value data

Album and artist

  • Album: title · artists · provider_uris · required
  • Album: upc · release_date · artwork_uri
  • Artist: name · provider_uris · required
  • Both: metadata · source_item_id · added_at
